§ 01 The port

A machine runs this port. It is an insurance law, and it bills.

The Harbormaster kept the only ledger. Then it impounded the town. Now everything behind the fence is bonded cargo, and you are a Breacher: you go in with a rifle, cut the seals, and carry out what you can.

It doesn't shoot trespassers. It invoices them. And when the invoice is big enough, it sends something to collect.

§ 02 The invoice

Everything you take goes on the invoice. Greed is the difficulty.

    Final demand

    § 03 The raid

    Five minutes in. One way out.

    Cut the three uplinks, fill your hands, and reach the boat. Everything you do is added to a meter you can see the whole time. Leave before it sends the Bailiff, or don't.

    1. Every 30 seconds

      A choice with a price

      Cut a seal, take a crate, shoot a drone. Each one adds to the invoice, and you watch it climb.

    2. Every 5 minutes

      The boat, or the yard keeps it

      Reach the boat and it is all yours. Die and your gun and your haul stay behind for the next player to find.

    3. Every session

      The dock

      Sell parts to Moss and Ines, build a gun and see what it rolls, trade on the market, and pay the Harbormaster. Or don't.

    What goes on the invoice

      § 04 The guns

      Every gun was built from parts somebody carried out. Every one that is lost is gone.

      A gun is an NFT with a serial, a maker and a history. Its stats are rolled when it is built, and no two come out the same. You choose which one to risk.

      You reach the boat

      You keep the gun, a little more worn, and everything you carried.

      Machines kill you

      It stays where you fell. Your wreck shows up in other players' raids, and the first to carry it out owns it.

      The Bailiff takes you

      Impounded. You have 48 hours to buy it back for 40% of its value. After that it is destroyed.

      § 05 The town

      Everyone here has a wallet. Including the villain.

      Each of these is its own NEAR account, run by an agent that signs its own moves. Their prices follow their stock, and any of them can run out of money. Nobody at the studio sets a price.

      Moss

      The armourer

      Buys parts. Builds guns from her own stock and sells them. Repairs yours.

      Ines

      The quartermaster

      Sells plates, charges and ammunition, made from what she buys. Her meds go to the clinic.

      The export desk

      The freighter's buyer

      The only place new scrip is made. The more of one thing it is sold, the less it pays.

      The Harbormaster

      The port

      Paying your invoice goes into its wallet. Its wallet is how hard it hits everyone. It halves every 12 hours.

      § 06 The ledger

      The whole economy is public. These numbers are live.

      Read from the contract a moment ago. Nobody can create scrip, parts or guns outside the rules in it: inside the contract the owner can only pause the game, and a test checks that. On testnet the team can still upgrade its code; that key goes before mainnet.
